# Design System: West Valley Rams Basketball x Rotary Crewneck

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Classic varsity athletics, cleaned up for screen print. Symmetric, centered chest and back placements (garment prints are read head-on, so the asymmetry rules for web layouts do not apply here). Density is low: one hero mark per side, two lines of type at most around it. No motion; this is a printed object.

- Variance: 2 (predictable, centered, garment-standard placements)
- Motion: 0 (print)
- Density: 3 (airy; generous fabric around each print)

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
West Valley colors are sampled from the district's arched wordmark and the Rams mascot art. Rotary colors are Rotary International's brand gold and royal blue.

- **Scarlet** (#A81028): outer keyline on all varsity lettering, ram outline
- **Columbia Blue** (#58A0D0): face color of varsity lettering, ram horns
- **Navy** (#0B1A36): slab type on light garments ("BASKETBALL", "SERVICE ABOVE SELF")
- **White** (#FFFFFF): inline between scarlet keyline and Columbia face; slab type on navy garments
- **Rotary Gold** (#F7A81B): basketball body and gear teeth on the back mark
- **Rotary Royal Blue** (#17458F): basketball seams, hub, gear keyline on light garments

Rules: Rotary colors appear only on the back mark and yoke logo. The front is West Valley only. Six spot colors maximum per garment; the front uses four (scarlet, Columbia, white, navy or white slab).

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Varsity display:** Graduate (stand-in for the beveled block letters in the West Valley wordmark). Always three layers: scarlet keyline (20% of cap height), white inline (8%), Columbia blue face. Set on an arch.
- **Slab support:** Zilla Slab Bold, tracked +5 to +6, all caps. Echoes the slab "HIGH SCHOOL" line under the official wordmark.
- **Banned:** script fonts, thin weights (they break up in screen print), more than two families per garment.

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Front (chest, 11 in wide):** arched "RAMS" in varsity layers, official ram head centered beneath, "BASKETBALL" in tracked slab under the ram.
- **Back (12 in wide):** Rotary-style basketball centered: a 24-tooth gear rim (from the Rotary wheel) around a ball with standard seams, finished with the wheel's keyed hub. "SERVICE ABOVE SELF" arches over it in slab; "WEST VALLEY" arches under it in varsity layers.
- **Back yoke:** official Rotary logo, 3.5 in wide, centered 2 in below the collar. Use Rotary's reversed (white) version on navy garments.
- **Garment colorways:** Heather grey (#C9CCD1) and navy (#16233F).

## 5. Layout Principles
- All prints centered on the garment's vertical axis.
- Chest print top sits 3 in below the collar seam; back print top sits 5 in below.
- Keep 1 in clear space between the gear teeth and any arched text.
- Arched text radius stays concentric with the back mark.

## 6. Production Notes
- Ram head and Rotary wheel must come from official vector art, not the web-resolution files used in this mockup.
- Confirm use of the West Valley marks with West Valley School District No. 208 and the Rotary marks with the sponsoring Rotary club (Rotary International's brand guidelines govern how the wheel can be used).

## 7. Anti-Patterns (Banned)
- No gradients or drop shadows in the print (spot colors only).
- No Rotary colors on the front.
- No distorting, recoloring or cropping the official ram head or Rotary wheel.
- No em-dashes in any printed text.
- No more than one mascot mark per side.
